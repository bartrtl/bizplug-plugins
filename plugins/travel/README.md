# BizPlug Travel & Tourism

Version 0.19.0. Tour and trip availability with price per traveller for travel & tourism businesses.

- Main function: Tour and trip availability with price per traveller
- MCP server URL: `https://mcp.bizplug.ai/plugin/travel` (streamable HTTP, no sign-in)
- A business's own plug-in: `https://mcp.bizplug.ai/plugin/travel/<business>`
- About this plug-in: https://bizplug.ai/industries/travel

## What a customer can do

- **Availability and price**: See what is free for your dates and party size, with the total price.
- **Request the booking**: Send a booking request. The business confirms it; nothing is held or charged here.
- **Ask a question**: Answers from the business's own information: packages, itineraries and travel enquiries.
- **Booking status**: Check a booking request with its reference.
- **Contact the business**: Phone, email, website and opening hours.

## Tools

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `show_menu` | Return this plug-in's menu: what the customer can do here, each with a short title, a one-line description and a question to try. Call it when the customer asks what this can do, asks for a menu or options, or opens without a question. ChatGPT shows the menu as a card with a button and a short form for each option; where there is no card, show the options exactly as returned (same titles, same order). Creates nothing. |
| `check_trip_availability` | Check which trip or tour options are free for the customer's dates and party size, with the published rate and total price. Creates nothing. |
| `request_trip_booking` | Send a booking request for the chosen trip or tour and dates to the business. Use only once the customer has chosen an option. The business confirms; nothing is held or charged in chat. |
| `get_booking_status` | Look up the status of a booking request by its reference. |
| `get_business_info` | Answer a general question about this travel & tourism business using only its published profile: packages, itineraries and travel enquiries. Creates nothing. If the profile does not cover the question, say so and use get_contact. |
| `get_contact` | Return the business's published contact details and opening hours for anything the other tools cannot do. |
